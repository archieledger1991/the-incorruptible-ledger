# the-incorruptible-ledger
An open-source, real-time AI ledger tracking UK political, corporate, and public authority conflicts of interest.

## 📢 1. VISION STATEMENT
Modern political, judicial, and local governance structures in the United Kingdom have broken their foundational social contract with the working class. Decisions impacting millions—from freezing tax thresholds to cutting vital community services or dropping criminal investigations—are routinely influenced by hidden, systemic conflicts of interest.

The mainstream media apparatus, consolidated under corporate and billionaire ownership, actively fails to hold power to account. Instead, it operates as an extension of the ruling elite, maintaining public ignorance through selective omission.

The Incorruptible Ledger is an automated, decentralized, algorithmic mirror designed to strip away this insulation. It bypasses corporate gatekeepers and career politicians by converting public records into a real-time, undeniable dashboard of institutional trajectory, financial greed, and performance metrics.

This system does not belong to a company, a government, or a single individual. It is a permanent, publicly owned asset running autonomously for the empowerment of the everyday citizen.

## 🔒 2. THE THREE UNBREAKABLE RULES
To ensure the system remains entirely free from external influence, manipulation, or corporate buyout, it is governed by three permanent, unalterable laws embedded directly into its open-source code:

*   **Rule I: Zero Manual Interference:** No human being—including developers, contributors, or public officials—can manually edit, delete, or obscure a profile, timeline, or data point once it is generated by the system.
*   **Rule II: Absolute Sourcing Integrity:** The system operates strictly as a forensic auditor of primary facts. It reads exclusively from live public APIs, official registries, and statutory datasets. It cannot ingest unverified rumors, opinions, or unsourced text.
*   **Rule III: Unconditional Open-Source Licensing:** The entire software framework is legally protected under an open public license (MIT). It cannot be bought, sold, monetized, or locked behind a private paywall.

## 🛠️ 3. THE CORE SOFTWARE SUITE
The backend engine is split into modular, automated data pipelines running independent forensic tracking scripts:

*   `scraper.py`: Connects directly to the live UK Parliament API to log raw baseline voting metrics and MP identities.
*   `landlord_tracker.py`: Scrapes the Parliamentary Financial Interests database to isolate and calculate real estate portfolio sizes and undeclared rental loops.
*   `corporate_links.py`: Cross-references MP registries against Companies House to expose directorships, shareholdings, and business assets held by politicians or their immediate family members.
*   `hospitality_clock.py`: Tracks Category 3 declarations (Gifts, Benefits, and Corporate Hospitality) to map timelines between accepted favors and subsequent committee votes.
*   `council_auditor.py`: Targets local town halls to trace and audit internal purchasing cards (P-Cards) against commercial capital losses.
*   `procurement_tracker.py`: Cross-references multi-million pound state contracts finder data against the vested financial interests of sitting legislators.
*   `public_authority_malpractice.py`: Monitors ground-level institutional overreach by scraping Local Government Ombudsman data and automated FOI patterns to log retaliatory administrative abuse (such as non-mandatory police referrals or fabricated social work notes).

## 🕸️ 4. SECURITY, INDESTRUCTIBILITY, AND AGENTIC DEPLOYMENT
Because the system presents the state's own data back to the public, it is legally insulated from standard censorship mechanisms. To ensure absolute survival against administrative or technical shutdown:

*   **Decentralized Architecture:** The system uses a decentralized file database structure (SQLite/IPFS) distributed across public nodes. If a domain is seized, the archive regenerates on a new node immediately.
*   **Agentic Automation:** The code blocks are structured to be fed directly into autonomous AI frameworks (such as Claude running via the Coinbase Base Model Context Protocol). The AI can self-fund its data requests, parse public records, and permanently etch verified authority misconduct directly onto the blockchain as an un-erasable, immortal public record.
